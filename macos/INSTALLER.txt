PocketRelay for macOS

1. Drag PocketRelay to Applications.
2. Open PocketRelay and choose your username and password.
3. Connect a provider account, create a key and copy the settings into your application.

No Docker, Homebrew, Python or Node.js required.
API: http://localhost:8788/v1
Closing the window keeps the service running in the menu bar.
"Quit and stop the API" stops the service.
Launch at login is optional and available in the app menu.

This local build is not notarized by Apple. macOS may block it from opening.
This repository does not provide a Developer ID or App Store package.

Docker remains available separately, with its own accounts and keys on port 8787.
